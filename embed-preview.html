NODE EDITOR

Build any terrain, node by node.

Start from a mountain, carve the valleys, let rain erode them, add snow and colour, and watch the terrain update as you connect each node. Every node shows a live preview, and only the nodes you change are computed again.

SIMULATION

Erosion that looks like a real place.

Water carves branching valley networks, sediment settles into the hollows, and thermal and glacial erosion shape cliffs, scree and U-shaped valleys. Below: the same six-node terrain before and after the simulation.

MADE WITH LITHOS TERRAIN

Landscapes that feel like places.

Rendered in Cycles. The terrain, its colours and rock come straight from the add-on, and so does the land around it out to the horizon (Surroundings); the dust and cloud layers and the colour grade were added for these shots.

SURROUNDINGS

The land goes on to the horizon.

A terrain is a square, and a camera that looks across it sees the ground stop. Surroundings carries it on: Build Final adds rings around the terrain, quilted from its own landforms at the same size and turned in every direction, each ring three times wider than the one inside it, up to forty terrain widths past every edge. They meet the terrain exactly and carry its colours, rock and haze.

LANDSCAPE NODE

One node. A whole landscape.

Pick a landform (mountain range, alpine massif, highland valley, rolling hills, canyon, volcano) and a climate (alpine, temperate, arid, cold), set the age, snow line, water and vegetation, and the Landscape node builds a complete terrain: shaped, eroded, coloured, with rock, snow, water, vegetation, flow and sediment masks. Landscape → Terrain Output is a finished graph.

ROCK

Rock that weathers like rock.

Rock Weathering breaks cliffs into beds, joints and blocks. Rock Layers adds hard and soft strata with dip and folds: linked into Stream and Thermal Erosion, hard beds stand as cliffs while soft beds fail into talus, for stepped canyon walls and scree aprons.

PRESET LIBRARY

135 ready-made terrains. All of them editable.

Canyons, glaciers, volcanoes, mesas, dunes, crater lakes, forests and highland valleys in 18 families. Each preset opens as a full node graph you can take apart and make your own.

ANY INPUT

Erode any mesh.

Object Height turns a model into terrain, Heightmap Image reads any height map, and Paint Mask lets you paint where things happen, right in the viewport. Then erode it like any other terrain.

EXPORT

Every map, ready for any engine.

Height, colour, normal, roughness, occlusion and every mask: 16-bit PNG, float OpenEXR or RAW, up to 8193 samples, tiled up to 8 × 8, with sizes for game-engine terrains and a JSON sidecar with the world size and height.
